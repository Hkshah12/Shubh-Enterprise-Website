Who We Are

Your Dedicated Laboratory
Supply Trading Partner

Shubh Enterprise was founded to simplify procurement for India's life sciences sector. We understand that the right supplies, delivered on time and to specification, are essential to the accuracy of diagnostics, the success of IVF programs, the safety of blood transfusions, and the integrity of pharmaceutical research.

We trade premium laboratory consumables, diagnostic reagents, IVF and ART supplies, blood banking products, and pharmaceutical testing materials — sourced from certified manufacturers and delivered with unmatched reliability across India.
