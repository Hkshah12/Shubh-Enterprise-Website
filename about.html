About Shubh Enterprise

Who We Are

A Dedicated Partner for Life Sciences Procurement

Shubh Enterprise was founded with a clear purpose: to simplify and strengthen the supply chain for laboratories across India. We understand that the right supplies, delivered on time and to specification, are not just a convenience — they are essential to the accuracy of diagnostics, the success of research, and ultimately, patient outcomes.

We specialise in trading high-quality laboratory consumables, diagnostic reagents, IVF and ART supplies, blood banking products, and pharmaceutical testing materials. Our team brings hands-on knowledge of the specific requirements of each sector we serve.

Whether you run a CRO handling multi-phase clinical studies, an IVF clinic managing embryo culture, a blood bank ensuring safe transfusions, or a pharma QC lab validating formulations — Shubh Enterprise is equipped to be your reliable procurement partner.
