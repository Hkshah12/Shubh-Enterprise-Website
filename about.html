Who We Are

A Dedicated Partner for Life Sciences Procurement

Shubh Enterprise was founded with a clear purpose: to simplify and strengthen the supply chain for laboratories across India. We understand that the right supplies — delivered on time and to specification — are not just a convenience; they are essential to the accuracy of diagnostics, the success of IVF programs, the safety of blood transfusions, and the integrity of pharmaceutical research.

We specialise in trading high-quality laboratory consumables, diagnostic reagents, IVF and ART supplies, blood banking products, and pharmaceutical testing materials. Our team brings hands-on knowledge of the specific requirements of each sector we serve, allowing us to go beyond simply supplying products — we advise, we customise, and we commit.

  • Products sourced from certified manufacturers
  • Sector-specific expertise and recommendations
  • Competitive, transparent pricing
  • Reliable pan-India delivery network
  • Dedicated after-sales support

Our Mission

To provide laboratories, clinics, and pharmaceutical companies across India with consistent access to high-quality, certified laboratory supplies — delivered reliably, priced fairly, and backed by expert guidance at every step.
