About Shubh Enterprise

Built on trust, quality and a deep understanding of India's life sciences sector.

Who We Are

A Dedicated Partner for Life Sciences Procurement

Shubh (शुभ) means auspicious. We were founded with a clear purpose: to simplify and strengthen the supply chain for laboratories across India. The right supplies, delivered on time and to specification, are not a convenience — they are the quiet precondition of accurate diagnostics, successful research and safe patient outcomes.

We specialise in trading high-quality laboratory consumables, diagnostic reagents, IVF and ART supplies, blood banking products and pharmaceutical testing materials — with hands-on knowledge of the specific requirements of each sector we serve.

Whether you run a CRO handling multi-phase clinical studies, an IVF clinic managing embryo culture, a blood bank ensuring safe transfusions, or a pharma QC lab validating formulations — Shubh Enterprise is equipped to be your reliable procurement partner.
